# Robocoins

Robocoins connects student learning progress, coin balances, and rewards with a personalized student identity.

## Language

**Goal**:
A reward selected by a student as their current savings target. Selecting a goal does not purchase or claim the reward.
_Avoid_: Purchase, redemption

**Avatar**:
The image representing a student, independent of its surrounding decoration.
_Avoid_: Avatar ring

**Avatar ring**:
A decorative effect associated with a student's avatar, which may surround the avatar or sit behind or below it.
_Avoid_: Avatar, badge

**Avatar effect**:
The interface term for an avatar ring when the decoration is presented as a visual effect. It refers to the same concept as the avatar ring.
_Avoid_: Ring, frame

**Locked avatar ring**:
An avatar ring displayed in the catalogue but unavailable for interaction or selection.
_Avoid_: Purchased ring, owned ring
